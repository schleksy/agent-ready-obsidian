# Inbox

## Purpose
Temporary capture whose destination is genuinely unknown. Known material should be filed through its existing canonical Hub.

## What belongs and what does not
Unresolved captures and their source attachments belong here. Permanent product truth, approved rules, and already processed captures do not.

## Read and write rules
Read [[99 System/Rules/Capture Workflow]] and [[99 System/Rules/Routing and Maintenance]]. During an organization pass, preserve this structural note and [[00 Inbox/Agent Inbox/Agent Inbox]]. Inventory all file types and re-list before closing. Mark only actual uncertainties unresolved.

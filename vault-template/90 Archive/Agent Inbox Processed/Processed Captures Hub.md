# Processed Captures Hub

## Purpose
One original envelope per processed capture, filed under its actual capture/source year where established. Unknown original dates must not be guessed.

## Rules
Follow [[99 System/Rules/Capture Workflow]]. Preserve capture identity and payload, processing state, destination links, and source dates. Move the existing record here; do not leave an active copy behind. No automatic deletion or fixed retention is configured.
